## DiceBear Micah avatars

Micah is a remix of **Avatar Illustration System** by **Micah Lanier**, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- Style and attribution: https://www.dicebear.com/styles/micah/
